Choose Bush or Fighter (Legacy) in Flight tuning. Switching profiles resets the flight; each keeps its own adjustments. Restore defaults resets the active profile and flight.

Use short, gentle inputs. Bank with A / D, then ease back with S to turn. Roll back to level as you finish.

Climbing and tight turns spend speed. Add throttle or lower the nose to rebuild it.

Stall phases describe the aerodynamic model; LOW AIRSPEED is a separate caution. RECOVERING means the stall effect is clearing, even if the aircraft is still descending.

Lower the nose with W and manage throttle. Once speed returns, ease out of the dive.

Use the wheel to zoom, or adjust camera distance and elevation in Flight tuning. Trees are scenery and have no collision.
